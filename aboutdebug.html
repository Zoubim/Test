About Us

Saporro Cafe opened its doors in Moorabbin with one simple goal: to serve honest, well-made coffee in a warm and welcoming space. Every cup is made to order, using freshly roasted beans and a lot of care.

All our baristas are experienced and passionate about their craft and were trained at the Barrista Course Melbourne
